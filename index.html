Teacher & Appraisal Profile

TPAD Teaching Standards

The current five-standard structure is represented here. Enter the appraisee, appraiser and agreed weights. Maximum weights are 24, 12, 9, 30 and 25 respectively, totaling 100. The app can later make these standards configurable.

Lesson Observation

Raw Lesson Observation score is out of 100. Each performance area is converted to a 1–5 Likert rating. The resulting lesson-observation rating is also shown as the TPAD Target 3 contribution on a maximum scale of 3.
